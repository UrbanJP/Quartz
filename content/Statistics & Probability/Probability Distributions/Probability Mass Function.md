

## Formal Definition
### Family Definition
Consider the [[Indexed Family|Family]] of numbers $\mathfrak{F} = \{f_k | k \in \mathbb{S}\}$ where $\mathbb{S} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^+$. Then $\mathfrak{F}$ is a probability density function [[Biconditional|if and only if]] $$f_k \geq 0 \qquad \forall k \in \mathbb{S} \qquad \text{and}  \quad \sum_{k \in \mathbb{S}}f_k = 1$$
