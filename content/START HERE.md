This note is meant to be an encyclopedia of sorts for mathematical definitions. The main purpose is to teach what math terms mean and how they connect to other fields of mathematics. This is not a replacement for a course or reading a textbook on any topic in mathematics. Instead it is meant to be a supplement, a reference to learn new mathematical terms or refresh on forgotten terms.
