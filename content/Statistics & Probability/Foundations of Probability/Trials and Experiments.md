An experiment is a procedure conducted mathematically with a set number of possible outcomes. A trial is defined relatively to an experiment where the entire experiment is made up of some number of trials. What counts as the experiment and the trial is determined by the experimenter based on what they are aiming to observe.

Take for example tossing three die. The procedure and outcomes of all the possible [[Combination|Combinations]] of the three die make up the experiment, but tossing one die can be considered a trial. But let's say the experimenter instead wanted to consider 100 three die combinations. Now the original experiment becomes a trial in a larger experiment. This is why there is no *objectively right way* to parse what counts as the experiment and the trial. Instead it is relative to the aims of the experimenter.
