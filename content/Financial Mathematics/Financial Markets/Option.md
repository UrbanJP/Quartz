The option financial asset is a financial [[Derivative (Finance)]] that gives someone the *option* to buy an underlying financial asset at a later time for a specific price. 

## Call and Put
A call option is the right to *buy* a financial product at [[Strike Price]] $K$ by the time of maturity $T$. A put option is the right to *sell* at [[Strike Price]] $K$ by the time of maturity $T$.

## Pricing an Option
There are several factors that affect the price of an option. The current stock price $S_0$, the [[Strike Price]] $K$, the time to expiration $T$, the volatility of the stock price $\sigma$, the risk-free [[Interest Rate]] $r$, and the [[Dividends]] that are expected to be paid.

Payout for European Put: $\text{max}(S_T - K, 0)$ 
Payout for European Call: $\text{max}(K-S_{T}, 0)$
