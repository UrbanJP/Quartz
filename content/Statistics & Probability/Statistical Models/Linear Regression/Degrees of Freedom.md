Degrees of Freedom is the number values that are free to vary without breaking any constraints of a system.

In general, it is the number of independent pieces of information minus the number of parameters you had to estimate from the data to compute the quantity you want the degrees of freedom of.
- Side Note: Revise definition
## Intuition
Consider three observations forming the [[Vector]] $(y_1, y_2, y_3)$. Since this vector could be anywhere in 3-dimensional space, it has three degrees of freedom.
