The present value of a specified amount of money is the [[Discount Rate|Discounted]] value of some future value.
$$ P_v = F_v \cdot \nu(t)$$
Where $\nu (t)$ is the [[Discount Rate]], the reciprocal of the [[Interest Rate]]. Sometimes it is denoted as
$$PV_{a(t)}(L \text{ at } t_0) = L \cdot \nu_{t_0}$$ where $L$ is the future value.
