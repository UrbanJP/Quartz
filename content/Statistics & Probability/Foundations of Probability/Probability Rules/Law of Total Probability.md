The law of total probability, also the total probability rule, says that the probability of A is the sum of the probabilities of its parts. 

## Formal Definition
Let $B_1, \ldots , B_n$ be a list of [[Disjoint Events]] in $\Omega$ such that $\bigcap_{i=1}^n B_i = \Omega$. Then for any event $A$, $P(A) = \sum_{i=1}^n P(A \cap B_i)$.
