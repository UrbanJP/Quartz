A sample space is the set of all possible outcomes of a [[Randomness|Random]] [[Trials and Experiments|Experiment]]. 

Each element in the sample space is called an outcome, however they are not events. Based on how a [[Probability Space]] is defined as a [[Measure Space]], [[Sigma Algebra|events]] are subsets of the sample space rather than elements of the sample space.

## Example
Consider a ball being under one of three cups; a red cup, a green cup, and a blue cup. We can define the sample space of this experiment to be the set $\{R, G, B\}$ where each letter represents the color of the corresponding cup. The event space could then be $\mathscr{P}( \{R,G,B \} )$ which is the power set.
