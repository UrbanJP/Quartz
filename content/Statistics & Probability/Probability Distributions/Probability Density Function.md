 The probability density function (PDF) of a [[Random Variable]] specifies the probability of the random variable being any range of values in the [[Sample Space]].

## Formal Definition
A probability density function of a [[Random Variable]] $X$ is a function $f(x)$ sometimes denoted as $f_X(x)$ such that $f(x) \geq 0$ and $\int_{- \infty}^{\infty}f(x)dx = 1$  for all $x$.
