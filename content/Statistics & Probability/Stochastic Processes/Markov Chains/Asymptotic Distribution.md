An asymptotic distribution describes the behavior of a [[Stochastic Process]] as some quantity $n$ approaches [[Infinity]]. It is often used interchangeably with [[Limiting Distribution]], a term that emphasizes how a stochastic process [[Convergence|Converges]] as $\lim_{n \to \infty}$, but the term asymptotic distribution can also be defined more broadly to mean the behavior in general whether the process converges or [[Divergence|Diverges]].
