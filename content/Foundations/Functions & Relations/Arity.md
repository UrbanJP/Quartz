Arity represents the amount of arguments a [[Function]], [[Relation]], [[Operator|Operation]], or [[Tuple]] has. For zero nullary, for one argument it is unary, for two binary or bivariate, for three ternary, for $n$ it is $n$-ary. Two or more is also sometimes called multivariate.

1. Unary
2. Binary | Bivariate
3. Ternary | Multivariate

For $n \geq 4$ [[Arguments]] the term $n$-ary or multivariate is used.

## Examplse
$f(x,y)$ is a bivariate function.

$R(a,b,c)$ is a ternary relation. An example of this kind of relation could be "$a$ is between $b$ and $c$".
