The multiplication rule of [[Probability]] states that
$$P(A_1 \cap A_2 \cap \ldots \cap A_n) = P(A_1) \cdot P(A_2 | A_1) \cdot \ldots \cdot P(A_n | A_{n-1} \cap \ldots \cap A_1)$$
$$P(A_1 \cap A_2 \cap \ldots \cap A_n | C) = P(A_1|C) \cdot P(A_2 | A_1 \cap C) \cdot \ldots \cdot P(A_n | A_{n-1} \cap \ldots \cap A_1 \cap C)$$
