Interest rate is [[Interest]] listed as a [[Rate]]. The two units are typically dollars owed in numerator and dollars borrowed in denominator. This is from the perspective of the borrower and not the lender.

## Examples
Let's say that someone borrows at an interest rate of $2.4 \%$. As a [[Ratio]] this is "$2.4:100$" meaning $2.4$ dollars owed for every $100$ dollars borrowed.
