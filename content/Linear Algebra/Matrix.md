A matrix is an array of items. An $M \times N$ (M by N) matrix is one with $M$ rows and $N$ columns. Below are two matrices $A$ and $B$. $A$ is a $2 \times 5$ matrix and $B$ is a $5 \times 2$ matrix.

$\qquad \qquad A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 13 & 0 & 0 \\ 20 & 3 & 2 & 2 &11 \end{bmatrix} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$  

A [[Square Matrix]] is any matrix where the number of rows is the same as the number of columns.

$$\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$$

To order the [[Element|Elements]] of a matrix we use $x_{ij}$ notation where this is the value of the $i$th row and $jth$ column. For any matrix, we can label the elements as such
$$\begin{bmatrix} x_{11} & x_{12} & \ldots  & x_{1j} & \ldots & x_{1n} \\  x_{21} & x_{22} & \ldots & x_{2j} & \ldots & x_{2n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots  & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ x_{i1} &  x_{i2} & \ldots & x_{i j} & \ldots  & x_{i n} \end{bmatrix}$$
