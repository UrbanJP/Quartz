A tuple can be thought of as extending the idea of an [[Ordered Pair]]. An ordered pair $(x,y)$ is a __pair__ where order matters. A tuple is the same without limiting the number of elements to two. The number of elements in the tuple is the [[Arity]] of the tuple.

## Formal Definition
An $n$-Tuple $(x_1, x_2, \ldots , x_n)$ is a finite [[Sequence]].
