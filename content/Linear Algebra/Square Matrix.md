A square matrix is a [[Matrix]] that has the same number of rows as columns. These matrices are often called $m \times m$ matrices denoting the [[Dimension]] of the Matrix.


## Examples
$$\text{A } 1 \times 1 \text{ matrix: } \quad \begin{bmatrix} 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
$$\text{A } 3 \times 3 \text{ matrix: } \quad 
\begin{bmatrix} a & b & c \\
d & e & f \\
g & h & i
\end{bmatrix}$$
