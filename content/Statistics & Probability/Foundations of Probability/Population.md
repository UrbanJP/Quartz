A population is each individual in the group that one wants to generalize over in [[Inferential Statistics]]. Since populations are so large and data collection would be impractical, statisticians use [[Sample|Samples]] of the population to do tests on and infer things about the population.


## Examples
Consider heights of people in the United States. The population would be every person in the United States. Since testing every single person in the U.S. is impractical, statisticians will take a sample of the entire population, for example 1000 people, and measure their heights. The group of 1000 people is called the sample.
