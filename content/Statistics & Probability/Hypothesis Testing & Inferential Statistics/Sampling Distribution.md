A distribution where each data point is a [[Sample Statistic]] such as an [[Arithmetic Mean]] or [[Variance]].
Consider a population of size $N$ and a sample of size $n<N$. The sampling distribution would be the distribution of all statistics from all samples of size $n$.


## Examples
Consider a population that consists of exactly ten possible samples of size $n$. The [[Sample Statistic|Sample Means]] of each sample for ten samples can be put into a list. Let's place these into a [[Sequence]] $(3,4,4,5,3,2,7,4,3,5)$. Since these are sample means, each mean corresponds to its own set of data that has its own [[Empirical Distribution]].
