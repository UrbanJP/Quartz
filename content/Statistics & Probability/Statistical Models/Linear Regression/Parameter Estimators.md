The parameter estimators $\hat \beta_0$ and $\hat \beta_1$ are [[Random Variable|Random Variables]] of a sample that estimate the true $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ of the population. $\hat \beta _0$ and $\hat \beta_1$ are variables because they change from sample to sample, but $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ are unknown [[Constant|Constants]].

The following are the equations for parameter estimators
$$\hat \beta_1 = \frac{SS_{xy}}{SS_{xx}}$$
$$\hat \beta_0 = \bar y - \hat \beta_1 \bar x$$
In vector form we can write the parameter estimators as normally distributed like such
$$\begin{pmatrix} \hat \beta_0 \\ \hat \beta_1 \end{pmatrix} \sim
N \begin{pmatrix} \ldots \end{pmatrix}$$
