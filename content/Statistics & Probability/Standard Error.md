Standard error is the [[Standard Deviation]] when sample statistics are treated as data points. A sample statistic is a measurement of a sample such as the mean of a sample. When a list of sample statistics are treated as data points themselves, this forms a [[Sampling Distribution]]. The standard error is a type of [[Standard Deviation]], but instead of being the standard deviation of data points in one sample, standard error is the standard deviation when considering multiple samples.

To further distinguish standard error and standard deviation, consider the population of people in the United States and their heights. Let's say we have ten samples of 1000 people taken from ten cities in the U.S., Chicago, Richmond, Los Angeles, Atlanta, Springfield, etc. We can ask what is the standard deviation of each sample? This is asking how much do the heights vary in each city. Upon answering this question we will get 10 answers, one for each city. But we can also ask, how do the means vary between each city? This question is about the standard error of sample means. For this question we get one answer which is the standard error.
