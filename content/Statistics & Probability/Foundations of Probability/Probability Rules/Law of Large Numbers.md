The law of large numbers states that the sample average $\bar x_n$ [[Convergence|Converges]] to the [[Expected Value]] of the [[Population]] ($\mu$) as $n \to \infty$. The difference between the strong law and the weak law is that from the weak law, $\bar x_n$ converges within a range regardless of how small. There is no direct equality statement. The strong law states that the convergence is equal.

## Formal Definition
### Strong Law of Large Numbers
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} P(| \bar x_n - \mu | < \varepsilon) = 1$$


### Weak Law of Large of Numbers
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} P(\bar x_n = \mu ) = 1$$
