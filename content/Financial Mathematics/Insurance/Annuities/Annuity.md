An annuity is an agreement between an insurance company and a policyholder where the policyholder pays in a lump sum or series of payments and the insurance company agrees to pay the policyholder over time starting at a future date. It is the core component for certain retirement plans.

Annuity can also refer to the loan structure. If a payment plan is structured like a [[Level Payment]] [[Annuity-Immediate]], then we can mathematically structure it as such without the actual financial product being an annuity.

For actuarial science exam FM, as of October 2026, when an annuity is mentioned, it is assumed to be a [[Level Payment]] annuity: an annuity with equal payments per pay period.

Contrast with [[Pension]].
