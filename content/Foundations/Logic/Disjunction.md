A disjunction represented by $\lor$ means inclusive "or" in ordinary language. In English, the term "or" has two related meanings, one inclusive and the other exclusive. Take the statement "$A$ or $B$". We can take the whole statement to be true when $A$ is true, $B$ is true, or both $A$ and $B$ is true. Then it is only false when $A$ and $B$ are both false. Let's call this interpretation 1. The second interpretation then is to take the whole statement to be true when either $A$ is true or $B$ is true. In the sense the whole statement is false when both $A$ and $B$ are false and when both $A$ and $B$ are true.

The disjunction $\lor$ follows interpretation 1.
