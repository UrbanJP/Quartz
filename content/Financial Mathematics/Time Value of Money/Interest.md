Additional money paid as compensation for a money service. This is often an annual percentage. Interest is the actual money amount, not the [[Interest Rate]]. 


## Examples
After borrowing $10,000 from a lender, the borrower pays back all the money lent. On top of this, the lender charges an extra 2% fee for lending out the money. This helps cover inflation as well as merely compensation for letting the borrower use their money. This additional 2% of the $10,000 which is $200 is the interest. The interest IS NOT 2%, the interest IS 2% OF $10,000. Instead, 2% is the [[Interest Rate]].

Banks need money to work with, so Superstar Bank opens up a savings account program with 4% annual percentage yield (APY) to attract customers. Tom opens up a new savings account with Superstar Bank and gains 4% interest on his deposit. Superstar Bank pays Tom 4% interest for letting them use his money.
