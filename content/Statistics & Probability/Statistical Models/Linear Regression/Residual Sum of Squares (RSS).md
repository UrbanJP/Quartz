The residual sum of squares (RSS), sum of squared residuals, or sum of squared estimate of error (SSE) all refer to the same thing. It measures how fit a model is to the data by calculating the sum of squared [[Residual|Residuals]], the difference between an observed value and the fitted value.

To measure the fitness of the [[Linear Regression]] model $y_i

$\Sigma_i r_i$ 
$$RSS = \sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - \beta_0 - \beta_1x_i)^2$$
