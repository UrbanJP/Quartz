Intersection is the analogue to [[Conjunction]] for propositions but instead applies to sets. For two sets $A$ and $B$, the intersection between the two notated as $A \cap B$ is the [[Set]] of all elements that are in both $A$ and $B$.


## Formal Definition
Let $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ be sets. The intersection
$$ \bigcap_{i=1}^n A_i = A_1 \cap A_2 \cap \ldots \cap A_n = \{x \,| \, (x \in A_1)\land (x \in A_2) \land \ldots \land (x \in A_n) \}$$
