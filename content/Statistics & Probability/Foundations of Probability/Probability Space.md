A probability space is a space with defined [[Event|Events]] based on possible outcomes and a probability function that assigns a probability to those events. The probability of any event will always be between 0 and 1 inclusively.

For certain spaces that mimic probability spaces but relaxes one or more of the [[Kolmogrov Axioms|Probability Axioms]] these are called [[Quasiprobability Spaces]].

## Formal Definition
A Probability Space is a [[Measure Space]] $(\Omega, F, P)$ where $\Omega$ is a [[Sample Space]], $F$ is a [[Sigma Algebra]] called the event space, and $P$ is a [[Function]] $P: F \to [0,1]$. This Measure Space also satisfies the [[Kolmogrov Axioms]].

Notice here there is no [[Random Variable]] $X$ defined in the [[Tuple]].
