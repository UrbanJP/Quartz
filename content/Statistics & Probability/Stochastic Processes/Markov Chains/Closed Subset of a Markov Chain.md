A closed subset of a [[Markov Chain]] is an extension of the [[Absorbing Property]] to multiple states instead of only one. Once you are inside you cannot get outside.

## Formal Definition
Consider a [[Markov Chain]] with [[State Space]] $\mathbb{S}$ and let $C \subseteq \mathbb{S}$. We say that $C$ is a closed [[Subset]] of a Markov Chain if $p_{jk} = 0 \quad \forall j \in C$ and $\forall k \not \in C$.
