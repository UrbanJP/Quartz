Total interest earned $I$ is the difference from the initial investment $K$ and the accumulated amount. This can also be composed into interest over a period of time $[t_n, t_m]$ where $t_n < t_m$ which represents the interest earned between times $t_n$ and $t_m$ denoted as $I_{[t_n, t_m]}$.

$I_n$ without a time span in the subscript is shorthand for $I_{[n-1, n]}$

## Formal Definition
For an initial investment $K$, a time $t$, and an [[Accumulation Function]] $a(t)$, the total interest earned denoted $I_{[0,t]}$ is defined as
$$I_{[0,t]} \equiv A_K(t) - K = K[a(t)-1]$$For interest earned over a period we have the following 
$$I_{[t_1, t_2]} \equiv A_K(t_2) - A_K(t_1) = K[a(t_2)-a(t_1)]$$
