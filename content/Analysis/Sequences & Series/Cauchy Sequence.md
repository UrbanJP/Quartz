A Cauchy Sequence is a sequence where the distance between each point approaches zero as the sequence continues

## Formal Definition
Given a [[Metric Space|Metric Space]] $(M,d)$, an infinite [[Sequence]] $a_n = x_1, x_2, \ldots$  is cauchy if and only if $\forall \epsilon \in \mathbb{R} > 0$ $\exists N \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $d(x_m, x_n) < \epsilon$ for all $m,n>N$. 

In other words, pick any real number $\epsilon$ however small that is greater than zero. After the $x_N$ term in the sequence, the distance between any two points $x_m$ and $x_n$ is less than $\epsilon$.
