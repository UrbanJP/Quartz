The geometric mean is a type of [[Mean]] where instead of adding the mean and dividing by the number of values, the values are multiplied and square rooted by the number of values.

## Formal Definition
Consider a list of values $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)$. Then the geometric mean is defined as
$$ \left( \prod_{i=1}^n x_i \right)^{1/n}$$
