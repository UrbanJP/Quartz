Not to be confused with a [[Measure Space]] or a [[Metric Space|Metric Space]]. A measurable space is a [[Set]] along with [[Subset|Subsets]] that can be measured.


## Formal Definition
A Measurable Space $(\Omega,F)$ is an ordered pair with a set $\Omega$ and a [[Sigma Algebra]] $F$. 

To contrast with a [[Metric Space|Metric Space]], a Measurable Space is an [[Ordered Pair]] of two sets $(\Omega, F)$ where $F$ functions like the [[Power Set]] $\mathscr{P}(\Omega)$. A metric space is an ordered pair $(M,d)$ where $d$ is a [[Function]]mapping $M^{2} \to \mathbb{R}$. A [[Measure Space]] is a measurable space alongside a measure.
