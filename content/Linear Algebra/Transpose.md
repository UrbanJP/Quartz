The transpose of a [[Matrix]] $A$ denoted as $A^T$ is the matrix constructed from swapping the rows and columns of $A$. The first column becomes the first row, second column second row, and so on. In other words, the $A_{ij}$ element becomes the $A_{ji}$ element.         

## Examples
Consider the matrix $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \\ 5 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$. Then $A^T = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 5 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \end{bmatrix}$.
