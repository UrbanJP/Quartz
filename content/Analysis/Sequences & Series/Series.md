A series is the [[Addition|Sum]] of all the terms in a [[Sequence]]. Some definitions limit sequences and series to the [[Infinity|Infinite]] case while other definitions allow for finite series and sequences. For my purposes, an infinite sequence will be part of the definition of a series.

## Formal Definition$
Given a sequence of elements $A$ with elements $a_i \in A$, a series $B$ would be the summation $\sum_{i \in I} a_i$ where $I$ is the set $\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, n \}$.
