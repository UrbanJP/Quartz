The Black-Scholes Model is a [[Parabolic Partial Differential Equation]] in which the Black-Scholes formula can be deduced. This formula is used to price European style [[Option|Options]]. The option has a unique price given its risk and expected return. The equation is the following:
$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2S^2 \frac{\partial^2V}{\partial S^2} = rV - rS \frac{\partial V}{\partial S}$$
where $V$ is the multivariate function $V(S,t)$ representing the price of the option, $S$ is the price of the underlying asset, and $t$ is time. Solving for $V$ gets us the price of the option.
