The amount function $A_k(t)$ represents the amount accumulated at time $t$ from investing $K$ dollars at time $t=0$. In the case where $k=1$ this is the [[Accumulation Function]]. The following formula utilizes both the amount and accumulation function
$$A_K(t) = K \cdot a(t)$$
where $K$ is the [[Principal]].


## Examples
Say we place $100 into an account at time $t=0$ and at $t=1$ and $t=2$ we have $110 and $125 respectively. then $A_{100}(1)$ and $A_{100}(2)$ equals $110 and $125.
