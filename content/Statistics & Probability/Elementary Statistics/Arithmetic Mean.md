The arithmetic mean is the usual sense of the term [[Mean]] or [[Average]]. It is found by taking the sum of all the values in the list then dividing by the number of [[Element|Elements]] in the list.


## Formal Definition
Consider a list of values $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)$. Then the arithmetic mean is defined as
$$ \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^nx_i$$
