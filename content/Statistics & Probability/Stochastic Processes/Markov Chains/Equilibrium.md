A [[Markov Chain]] is said to be in equilibrium if all [[Random Variable|Random Variables]] in the Markov chain $Y_0, Y_1, \ldots , Y_n$ are [[Independent and Identically Distributed (IID)|Identically Distributed]]. Equilibrium does not require [[Independence]] so it does not require the random variables to be [[Independent and Identically Distributed (IID)]]. Note that all random variables in the chain have to be identically distributed without exception. If the random variables become identically distributed later in the chain then the Markov chain as a whole is not in equilibrium.
