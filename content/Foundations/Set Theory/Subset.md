Let $A$ be any set. $B$ is a subset of $A$ [[Biconditional|if and only if]] all the elements in $B$ are in $A$. This is denoted as $B \subseteq A$. If the subset is proper it is denoted as $B \subset A$. A subset is proper if the [[Cardinality]] of the subset is less than the cardinality of the original set in consideration.

The analogue to these symbols for numbers are the $\leq$ and $<$ symbols.

- $A \subseteq B \implies |A| \leq |B|$

- $A \subset B \implies |A| < |B|$
