A biconditional $(\iff)$ is a two-way [[Implication]]. It means "if and only if" and is sometimes shortened to "iff". 

## Examples
$A \iff B$
- $A$ if and only if $B$
- $A$ iff $B$

The above biconditional is true only if $A \implies B$ and $B \implies A$ are both true. It also shows that $A$ and $B$ are [[Equivalent]] propositions.
