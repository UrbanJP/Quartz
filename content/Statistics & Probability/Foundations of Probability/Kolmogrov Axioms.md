The Kolmogrov Axioms are the axioms foundational to probability theory and probability spaces

## The Three Axioms
$$1. \quad \forall E \in F, \quad P(E) \geq 0$$
$$2. \quad P(\Omega) = 1$$
$$3. \quad \bigcap_{i=1}^{n}E_i = \emptyset \implies P \left( \bigcup_{i=1}^{n}E_i \right) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}P(E_i)$$
For the third axiom $n$ is in the [[Set]] of [[Extended Real Numbers]]. The first axiom states that there are no negative probabilities for events. The second axiom says that some event in the [[Sample Space]] has to occur. The third axiom is the [[Additive Rule of Probability]] for disjoint events.
