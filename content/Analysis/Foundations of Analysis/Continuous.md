A [[Function]] is continuous if you can draw it without lifting your pencil


## Formal Definition
A function can be continuous with respect to a [[Point]] or as a whole. A function $f: \mathbb{R}^n \to S$ is continuous at a point $(a_1, a_2, \ldots , a_n)$ if $$\lim_{(x_1,\ldots , x_n) \to (a_1, \ldots , a_n)} f(x_1, \ldots , x_n) = f(a_1, \ldots , a_n)$$
This creates the single [[Variable]] condition $$\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$$
A function is continuous as a whole if this limit condition is met for all points in the [[Domain]].
