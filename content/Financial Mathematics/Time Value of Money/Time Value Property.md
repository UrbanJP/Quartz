The time value property is a property about calculating the [[Amount Function]] at time $t$ from the amount and [[Accumulation Function|Accumulation]] at a previous time $s$. The amount at time $t$ is the amount at time $s$ multiplied by the accumulation of $t-s$.

## Formal Defintion
$\forall t > s, \, A_K(t) = A_K(s) \cdot a(t-s)$
