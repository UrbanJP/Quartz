Beta is the systematic risk or market risk of an asset.


## Formal Definition
Market beta is defined to be the value of $\beta_i$ in the [[Linear Regression]] 
$$ r_{i,t} = \alpha_i + \beta_i (r_{m,t}) + \varepsilon_t$$ where $i$ is the asset, $t$ is the number of observed occasions, and $r_{i,t}$ is the rate of return on $r_{m,t}$.
$r_{m,t}$ is the rate of return of the index $m$. $\alpha_i$ is the [[Alpha (Finance)]] and $\varepsilon_i$ the [[Error]].

By [[Ordinary Least Squares]] we get $$\beta_i = \frac{Cov(r_i, r_m)}{Var(r_m)}$$
