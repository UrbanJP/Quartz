Curvature is the measure of how fast a curve is changing direction at a particular point


## Formal Definition
Let $\vec{T}$ be the [[Unit Tangent Vector]] and $s$ be the [[Arclength]] of a curve. The curvature is represented as 
$$k = \left| \left| \frac{d \vec{T} }{ds} \right| \right|$$
The following two equations can be derived from the above
$$k = \frac{||\vec{T}'(t)||}{||\vec{r}'(t)||}$$
$$k = \frac{|| \vec{r}'(t) \times \vec{r}''(t)||}{||\vec{r}'(t)||^3}$$
