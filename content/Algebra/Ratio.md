A ratio is a way to compare two or more numbers. 

## Examples
Say for example we have a chemical solution mixing water and oil where we add two cups of water for every cup of oil. The ratio of water to oil would be $2:1$, or $\frac{2}{1}$. Alternatively we could list the ratio of oil to water as $1:2$.

Adding on to the previous example, let's say for every cup of oil we add half a cup of alcohol, a quarter cup of flour, and three cups of chlorine. When listing ratios it is best practice to list everything as [[Integers|Integers]]. We would write the ratio of chlorine, water, oil, alcohol, and flour as $12:8:4:2:1$.
