A [[Z-Score]] is a [[Test Statistic]] that allows for [[Hypothesis Test|Hypothesis Testing]] with the [[Z-Test]]. It is a form of [[Standardizing]] a [[Normal Distribution]]. There are three separate formulas derived from a general formula. The general Z-score formula is
$$Z = \frac{\text{estimator } - \text{ null value}}{\text{standard error of the estimator under } H_0} $$


The equation for finding the Z-score for comparing a sample mean and population mean is $$Z = \frac{\bar{x} - \mu_0}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}} $$where $\bar{x}$ is the [[Sample Statistic|Sample Mean]], $\mu_0$ is the hypothesized mean, $\sigma$ is the [[Population]] [[Standard Deviation]], and $n$ is the sample size. Applying this equation is called [[Standardizing]].
