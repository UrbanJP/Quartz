The p-Value is the probability of a [[Test Statistic]] being at least as extreme as the one obtained. 

## p-Value Method
With the p-Value we can utilize the p-Value method for determining to accept or reject a [[Null Hypothesis]]. If the p-Value is less than the selected [[Significance Level]] $\alpha$, then we reject the null hypothesis. If the p-Value is greater than $\alpha$, we fail to reject the null hypothesis.
