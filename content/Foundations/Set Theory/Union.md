Union is the analogue to a [[Disjunction]] but instead of being for [[Proposition|Propositions]] it is for a set. The union between two sets $A$ and $B$ notated as $A \cup B$ is the set of all elements that are in $A$ or $B$. The union between $n$ sets $A_1, A_2, \ldots , A_n$ is the union $A_1 \cup A_2 \cup \ldots \cup A_n$.

There is a more concise notation for the union of multiple sets. These include the following:
$$ \bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i \qquad \bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty}A_i \qquad \bigcup_{i \in I}A_i$$
The first is the union of $n$ sets. The second, the union of an infinite number of sets. The third is the union of all sets $A_i$ where $i$ is an element of the [[Index Set]] $I$.
